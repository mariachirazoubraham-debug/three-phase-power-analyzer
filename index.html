Engineering Formulas

Apparent Power

S = √3 × VL × IL

Unit: VA

Active Power

P = √3 × VL × IL × cosφ

Unit: W

Reactive Power

Q = √3 × VL × IL × sinφ

Unit: VAR

Star Connection

Vph = VL / √3
Iph = IL

Delta Connection

Vph = VL
Iph = IL / √3

Power Factor

cosφ = P / S

0 ≤ cosφ ≤ 1
